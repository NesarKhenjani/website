How Soccer Started

Soccer, or association football, began over 2,000 years ago with ancient kicking games like Cuju in China.

Modern soccer developed in 19th century England. FIFA's website

Soccer Facts

  • Each team has 11 players.
  • A game is usually 90 minutes.
  • Soccer is played around the world.
  • Players cannot use their hands except the goalkeeper.
